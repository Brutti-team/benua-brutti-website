Benua Brutti designs and builds functional pieces in Sabah, combining hands-on craft, practical problem-solving and a commitment to giving useful materials a longer life.

Who we are

Built with purpose,
made in Sabah.

Brutti grew from a hands-on DIY journey into a team that creates furniture, commercial builds, custom pieces and practical solutions. The work is grounded in local identity, useful design and continuous learning.

01 · Visi

Angkat Reka Bangsa, Tawan Dunia.

We aim to carry Malaysian design and Sabah-made craftsmanship further through work that is useful, distinctive and built to last.

02 · Misi

Cipta yang cantik, buat yang berguna.

We create with practical value in mind, balancing function, craft and the character of the materials we work with.
